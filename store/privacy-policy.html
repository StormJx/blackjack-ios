二十一点 · 隐私说明

应用版本 2.0 · 更新日期 2026-08-30 · 本页与应用内「隐私说明」一致

数据

本应用不创建账号，不收集个人身份信息，不使用追踪技术，也不向第三方发送对局数据。

战绩、成就、关卡进度、设置与外观仅使用本机存储（系统用户默认设置）。卸载应用将删除这些数据。

当前版本不含广告软件开发包，也不会在对局进行中弹出广告。

本应用为模拟筹码的二十一点练习，不含真实货币赌博。将按模拟赌博相关规则申报商店年龄分级（17+）。

玩法

产品以公平闯关为主；娱乐模式的道具只影响该模式，不改变闯关赔率与牌值。

日后若接入广告或统计，将更新本页、应用内说明与 App Store 隐私标签。

Blackjack · Privacy

App version 2.0 · Updated 2026-08-30 · Same wording as the in-app Privacy screen

Data

This app has no accounts, does not collect personal identity data, does not track you, and does not send hand data to third parties.

Stats, achievements, stage progress, settings, and cosmetics are stored only on this device (system user defaults). Deleting the app removes this data.

This version includes no ad SDK and will not show ads during a hand.

This is a practice blackjack game with simulated chips, not real-money gambling. The App Store age rating follows simulated-gambling guidelines (17+).

Gameplay

The product is fair-challenge first. Entertainment props affect that mode only and never change Challenge odds or card values.

If ads or analytics are added later, this page, the in-app note, and App Privacy labels will be updated together.
